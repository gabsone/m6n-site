How it works

Three steps. The differentiator is step two: the template is yours — not ours.

  1. 1

    Write in Markdown

    Syntax highlighting, search, and live preview so you stay in flow — even for long documents.

  2. 2

    Attach your Word template

    Point m6n at your model: headings, styles, and layout follow the rules encoded in your DOCX — brand guidelines, faculty template, client boilerplate.

  3. 3

    Export DOCX

    One action: a file ready for review, sharing, or archival in Word — without reformatting by hand.

Security & privacy

Built for people who care where text lives and what phones home.

  • Stays on your machine

    Editing and conversion run locally. Your manuscript is not uploaded to our servers — because there is no “our cloud” in the loop.

  • No telemetry in the core

    The project ships without analytics or tracking in the core path. Your content is not a product.

  • Templates stay yours

    Templates live in your library on disk. You choose what to use; nothing is forced from a remote catalog.

  • Small attack surface

    A compact native shell (Tauri) instead of a full browser stack keeps the app lighter and easier to reason about.

Who it’s for

Anyone who likes Markdown but must deliver Word that matches an existing template.

  • Organizations

    Internal reports and memos that must match corporate Word styles — without pasting into a blank document.

  • Freelancers & studios

    Draft fast in Markdown; hand off DOCX that already follows the client’s template.

  • School & research

    Notes in Markdown; submissions in the official Word layout when required.

Stack

Modern UI, Rust conversion pipeline — chosen for responsiveness and a smaller footprint than typical Electron apps.

  • Tauri 2 Lightweight native shell
  • Svelte 5 Fast, minimal UI runtime
  • Rust Markdown → DOCX conversion
  • CodeMirror 6 Text editor engine
