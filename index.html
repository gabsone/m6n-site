How it works

Three steps. The differentiator is step two: the template is yours — not ours.

  1. 1

    Write or paste Markdown

    Compose in the editor, or paste output from another tool — including long drafts from an AI assistant. Syntax highlighting, search, and live preview keep you oriented.

  2. 2

    Attach your template

    Point m6n at your template file: headings, styles, and layout follow the rules encoded in your DOCX — brand guidelines, faculty model, client boilerplate.

  3. 3

    Export DOCX

    One action: a file ready for review, sharing, or archival — without reformatting by hand.

From AI-generated Markdown to polished DOCX — fast

Large language models are great at producing Markdown. Turning that into a client-ready or policy-compliant DOCX is usually slow: copy-paste, fix headings, fix styles, fix spacing — again and again.

  • The bottleneck isn’t the draft

    It’s mapping unstructured or semi-structured Markdown onto your official template. That manual step doesn’t scale when you iterate with AI often.

  • m6n closes the gap

    Keep the speed of generating in Markdown; let m6n apply your template on export so the DOCX already matches house style — in a fraction of the time you’d spend fixing formatting by hand.

  • Same privacy story

    Conversion runs on your computer. You choose what leaves your machine; m6n doesn’t require you to ship your manuscript through a third-party formatter to get a templated DOCX.

Security & privacy

Built for people who care where text lives and what phones home.

  • Stays on your machine

    Editing and conversion run locally. Your manuscript is not uploaded to our servers — because there is no “our cloud” in the loop.

  • No telemetry in the core

    The project ships without analytics or tracking in the core path. Your content is not a product.

  • Templates stay yours

    Templates live in your library on disk. You choose what to use; nothing is forced from a remote catalog.

  • Lean by design

    A small, focused app keeps things simple: less surface area, less to trust — and a download that stays modest.

Who it’s for

  • Organizations

    Internal reports and memos that must match corporate styles in the template — without pasting into a blank document.

  • Freelancers & studios

    Draft fast in Markdown; hand off DOCX that already follows the client’s template.

  • AI-assisted writers

    Iterate in Markdown with your favorite assistant, then export to templated DOCX without a formatting marathon.

  • School & research

    Notes in Markdown; submissions in the required DOCX layout when the institution asks for it.
